<p>TrenoLibero is a free and unofficial client for the Italian railway real-time data published by ViaggiaTreno (RFI/Trenitalia). It shows the same information as the website in a lightweight app without ads or tracking.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Train status</b>: search by number to see every stop with scheduled, actual and expected times, delay, last detection point, scheduled and actual platforms, cancelled stops.</li>
<li><b>Station board</b>: real-time departures and arrivals with delays and platforms, also from a chosen time.</li>
<li><b>Map</b> (OpenStreetMap): all stations and the train route with its last known position.</li>
<li><b>Travel info</b>: service disruption notices and number of trains running.</li>
<li><b>Favourites</b> stored only on the device.</li>
</ul>
<p>Not affiliated with or endorsed by Trenitalia, RFI or FS Group. Data comes from the viaggiatreno.it public service, which is not free software (NonFreeNet anti-feature).</p>
